Historical Fraud Investigation Case 001

Case Type:
International transfer

Transaction Amount:
INR 92000

Location:
Dubai

Customer Risk:
Medium

Observed Behavior:
Customer normally performs domestic transactions below INR 10000.
A large international transfer was initiated suddenly.

Fraud Indicators:
- Unusual transaction amount
- International location
- Transaction behavior inconsistent with historical activity

Investigation Outcome:
Transaction was placed under enhanced review.
The transaction was not automatically approved.

Risk Assessment:
High risk due to unusual amount, international location,
and deviation from normal customer behavior.
